Hi, I'm Timur, and I built TradePerpl.

Perpl gives traders an onchain perpetual exchange on Monad, but its official experience is web-first. When you're away from a desk, checking markets, managing risk, or reacting to a position should not require navigating a full trading terminal.

TradePerpl brings the essential workflow into Telegram: live markets and funding, charts and order books, price alerts, portfolio and positions, and guided order entry with leverage, take profit, and stop loss.

Trades use revocable Perpl API keys that cannot withdraw funds. Orders and position updates arrive through Perpl's real-time API, while settlement happens on Monad Mainnet.

I built TradePerpl to turn Telegram from a notification channel into a focused trading interface: fast to open, familiar on mobile, and available wherever traders already communicate.
